How the LFP Payment Model Works

The Longitudinal Family Physician (LFP) Payment Model compensates family physicians for all aspects of longitudinal care. LFP pays you for direct patient care, indirect charting and lab review, clinical administration, patient visit top-ups, and maintaining an attached panel.

The 3 Core Pillars of LFP Compensation

1

Time Payments

Earn $130.00 / hour ($32.50 per 15-minute unit) for all continuous work performed in 3 distinct categories:

  • 98010 Direct Care: In-person or real-time virtual care with patient present.
  • 98011 Indirect Care: Charting notes, reviewing labs/diagnostics, consult notes, prescription refills.
  • 98012 Clinical Admin: Practice panel management, QI, clinic operations (max 10% annual limit).
2

Patient Interactions

Flat-rate top-ups billed IN ADDITION to time codes whenever an encounter or procedure takes place:

  • 98031 In-Person Clinic Visit: +$25.00 top-up
  • 98032 Virtual Visit (Phone/Video): +$25.00 top-up
  • 98021/20 Standard / Advanced Procedure: +$60.00 / +$110.00
  • 98022 Minor Proc / Diagnostic Add-on: +$10.00 (max 2/pt)
3

Panel Payments

Automatic quarterly payments based on attached panel size in the Provincial Attachment System (PAS) and patient complexity:

  • • Complexity-Adjusted: Paid higher rates for elderly, frail, or multi-morbid attached patients.
  • • Minimum Requirement: Must attach at least 250 patients within 4 months of LFP practice start.
  • • Automated Remittances: Paid directly by MSP every quarter based on active PAS registry data.

Key LFP Clinical Workflow Benefits

Unpaid Charting Eliminated

All evening charting, inbox lab reviews, and specialist consult letters are compensated at $130/hr under code 98011.

Complex Care Rewarded

A 40-minute complex visit yields 2 units of direct time ($65.00) + visit top-up ($25.00) = $90.00 total.

Non-Panel Flexibility

Up to 30% of total annual clinic time can be spent treating unattached or walk-in patients under full LFP rates.

Seamless Locum Coverage

Locums use designated codes (98040–98064) to bill exact LFP rates without disturbing panel payments.

Traditional FFS vs LFP Payment Model

Side-by-side workflow comparison showing how patient care, charting, and indirect hours are compensated.

Traditional Fee-For-Service (FFS)

Volume-Driven
  • ✕ Unpaid Charting & Inbox: Time spent reviewing lab results, consult reports, and filing EMR charts earns $0.00.
  • ✕ Complex Patient Penalty: Spending 45 minutes on a complex multi-morbidity visit earns the exact same flat fee (~$32-$40) as a 5-minute visit.
  • ✕ No Administrative Funding: Panel management, quality improvement, and clinic management are unpaid.
  • ✕ Locum Splits: Locums require complex fee-split negotiations (e.g. 70/30 split).

New LFP Payment Model

Comprehensive Care
  • ✓ Paid Charting & Inbox ($130/hr): Every 15 minutes of indirect care is paid under code 98011.
  • ✓ Time-Proportional Visits: Longer complex visits earn time units ($32.50/15m) + interaction top-up (+$25.00).
  • ✓ Funded Panel Admin: Panel reviews and practice QI are billable under code 98012.
  • ✓ Direct Locum Billing: Locums bill designated codes (98040–98064) directly under their own practitioner number.

Key LFP Billing Rules You Must Follow

1. The 15-Minute Rounding Down Rule Time units are calculated per continuous block and always rounded down to the nearest 15-minute increment (e.g. 28 min = 1 unit; 30 min = 2 units).
2. Daily & Rolling Time Limits Maximum allowable time across all time codes combined is 14 hours (56 units) per calendar day and 120 hours in any 14-day rolling window.
3. The 10% Clinical Admin Threshold (98012) Clinical admin time (98012) should not exceed 10% of your total annual clinical hours.
4. WorkSafeBC (WCB) Exclusions Services for WorkSafeBC claims CANNOT be billed under LFP time codes. Deduct WCB time from LFP time blocks and bill WCB separately under FFS.
5. Out-of-Province Visitors LFP time codes apply only to BC PHN holders. Bill out-of-province patients under standard FFS visit codes (e.g., 00100 for office visit).

MSP Explanatory Codes & How to Fix Them

PM Missing Same-Day Explanation

Triggered when multiple same-day LFP encounters are submitted without an accompanying note explaining why distinct interactions were required.

PS Invalid Procedure Combination

Triggered when a standalone procedure is billed with a visit code that already includes procedural compensation.

PT Duplicate Procedure Limit

Triggered when more than two minor procedure codes (98022) are submitted for the same patient on the same calendar day.

PN Overlapping Time Blocks

Triggered when Teleplan claim start/end times overlap with another time code submitted by you or your locum.

Step-by-Step LFP Setup Guide

Follow this checklist to activate LFP billing privileges with MSP and the Provincial Attachment System (PAS).

1

Submit Portal Enrolment Code (98000 / 98005)

Submit fee code 98000 (Locums submit 98005) via Teleplan using the official dummy patient demographics:

PHN: 9694105066
Surname: Portal
First Name: LFP
Date of Birth: Jan 01, 2023
ICD-9 Code: L23
2

Register Panel in the PAS Portal

Log into the Health Sector Locator / PAS registry. Upload your active patient list to ensure quarterly complexity-adjusted panel payments are triggered. You must reach a minimum of 250 attached patients within 4 months.

3

Configure EMR Time Macros

Set up quick-select time codes (`98010`, `98011`, `98012`) and interaction codes in OSCAR, Med Access, MOIS, or Profile EMR. Ensure daily start and end times are recorded for audit compliance.

4

Submit Claims Within 90 Days

Submit all LFP time and interaction claim records to Teleplan within 90 days from the service date.
